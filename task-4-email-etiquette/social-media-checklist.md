# 📱 Social Media Do’s. Don’ts Checklist

As a college student you need to use social media in a way that is responsible and professional. Here are some things you should do and some things you should not do when you are on media.

## ✅ Do’s

- You should use language that's professional and respectful when you post something or make a comment on social media.

- Make sure your profile looks good and is appropriate for everyone to see this means having a good bio and profile picture.

- Before you post something think about it for a moment. Would you be okay if a teacher or a person who might hire you saw it?

- You should share things that're useful, positive and give people good information.

- Use the privacy settings on media to control who can see the things you post.

## ❌ Don’ts

- Do not post things that're offensive, abusive or might cause trouble.

- You should not share things like your phone number, address or passwords on social media.

- Do not get into fights with people do not troll and do not bully anyone on the internet.

- Do not spread information or things that you are not sure are true.

- Do not post anything that could hurt your reputation or make it hard for you to get a job in the future.

## 📌 Note

When you use media in a responsible way it helps you build a good digital identity and it protects you from bad things that can happen online. Social media usage is very important for college students like you so you should always use media responsibly and professionally this will help you a lot in the long run and social media will be a useful tool, for you.

# 📱 Social Media Do’s and Don’ts Checklist

As a college student, it is important to use social media responsibly and professionally. Below are some key Do’s and Don’ts to follow:

---

## ✅ Do’s

- Use **professional and respectful language** in your posts and comments  
- Keep your **profile clean and appropriate** (good bio and profile picture)  
- Think before posting — *“Would I be okay if a teacher or employer sees this?”*  
- Share **useful, positive, and informative content**  
- Maintain **privacy settings** to control who can view your posts  

---

## ❌ Don’ts

- Don’t post **offensive, abusive, or controversial content**  
- Avoid sharing **personal information** like phone number, address, or passwords  
- Don’t engage in **online fights, trolling, or cyberbullying**  
- Avoid spreading **fake news or unverified information**  
- Don’t post anything that can **damage your reputation or future career**  

---

## 📌 Note
Responsible social media usage helps in building a positive digital identity and protects you from online risks.
